Deposits at 36 — up 20% vs last year (30). Best early-cycle deposit count in the four-year window. Up from 29 last week (+7 in one week). The deposit trajectory is the strongest positive story in the cycle and a leading indicator for final enrollment.
Submitted apps gap narrowed to -5.5% (1,325 vs 1,402 last year) — from -5.4% last week, essentially holding. Accepts at -9.0% (-91 apps) is the current processing bottleneck. Created accounts up 90% (19 vs 10 last year) — a strong leading indicator for future started apps.

Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. YTD deposits (36) represent the early cycle only — the vast majority arrive later. Prior year totals ranged from 402–495 final deposits, meaning current YTD is approximately 7% of where the year will end.

The story for leadership

Fall 2027 has the highest early-cycle deposit count (36), highest start-to-submit conversion rate (92.5%), and the most created accounts (19, +90%) in the four-year window. Submitted apps are within 77 of last year (-5.5%). The started apps gap (-16.2%) is real and needs sustained top-of-funnel attention, but every other funnel metric tells a story of higher-intent students converting at a record rate.
