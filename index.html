Fall 2027 active Deposits +16% vs LY Started apps -18.4%
💰
Deposits hit 29 — up 16% vs last year (25). Best early-cycle deposit count in the four-year window by a significant margin. Current deposits at +16% is the strongest positive metric in the report and a meaningful indicator for the final enrolled class.
Submitted apps gap now just -5.4% (1,166 vs 1,232 last year). Three weeks ago submitted apps were -7.7% behind. Accepts at -5.9% also nearly at parity. The conversion funnel is operating at a record 92.5% start-to-submit rate — the strongest in four years.
Started apps gap at -18.4% (1,261 vs 1,546 last year) remains the key challenge. +225 started since 10/12 (+17.8%) — strong single-week movement. The gap is closing but will need sustained top-of-funnel pressure through spring recruitment to fully recover.
Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. YTD deposits (29) are an early-cycle indicator — the vast majority arrive later. At +16% vs last year, this is the highest early-cycle deposit count in the four-year window.

The deposit and conversion story for leadership

Fall 2027 has the highest early-cycle deposit count (29) and the highest start-to-submit conversion rate (92.5%) in the four-year window. Submitted apps are within 66 of last year's pace (-5.4%). The started apps gap (-18.4%) is real but the downstream metrics suggest the students who are applying are significantly higher-intent than prior years — meaning the final enrolled class may land closer to last year than the top-of-funnel gap implies.
