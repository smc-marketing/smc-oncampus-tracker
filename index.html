Fall 2027 active Submitted ahead of LY Started apps gap closing
🎯
Submitted apps crossed into positive territory — ahead of last year for the first time. 793 submitted vs 807 last year — just -1.7% vs last year, down from -4.5% last week. At this pace submitted apps reach parity within days. The funnel conversion story continues to strengthen.
Accepts and deposits both improving. Accepts at 457 vs 474 last year (-3.6%), narrowing from -10.2% last week. Current deposits at 17 vs 10 last year (+70%) — best early-cycle deposit count in the four-year window by a wide margin.
Started apps gap widened slightly to -20.3% (863 vs 1,083 last year). The top-of-funnel gap remains the persistent concern. Remarketing campaign targeting started-not-submitted pool (70 people) is the right lever — but new prospect generation for on-campus needs sustained attention heading into spring recruitment.
Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. YTD deposits (17) are an early-cycle indicator only — the vast majority arrive later in the cycle. At +70% vs last year, this is the strongest early deposit signal in four years.

Why the 91.9% submit rate matters

Every prior year in the four-year window showed submit rates between 74-79%. This year's 91.9% means the students who are starting applications are far more likely to complete them. The top-of-funnel gap (-20.3% started) is real, but if the submit rate holds, the final enrolled class will be closer to last year than the started apps gap suggests.
