Submitted apps crossed into positive territory — now ahead of last year. 959 submitted vs 949 last year (+1.1% vs last year). Three weeks ago submitted apps were -7.7% behind. The funnel conversion rate of 92.6% is the highest in the four-year tracking window by a wide margin.
Deposits ahead of last year for the first time. 21 current deposits vs 20 last year (+5%). At +75% vs two weeks ago (12→21), deposit momentum is building. Accepts at -7% vs last year (-42 apps) remain the primary bottleneck to closing the deposit gap further.

Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. YTD deposits (21) are an early-cycle indicator — the vast majority arrive later. At +5% vs last year, this is the best early-cycle deposit position in the four-year window.

The 92.6% submit rate in context

Every prior year in the four-year window showed submit rates between 75-82%. This year's 92.6% means nearly every student who starts an application completes it — the strongest funnel conversion rate tracked. This partially offsets the started apps gap and suggests the students being recruited this cycle are higher-intent than in prior years.
