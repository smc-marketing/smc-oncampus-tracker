Fall 2027 active Funnel conversion strong
Funnel conversion is the bright spot. Despite started apps down 26.1%, submitted apps are only -7.7% vs last year — meaning students who start are completing at a much higher rate than before. Accepts are nearly at parity (-5.8%). The top-of-funnel (started apps) is the primary gap to close.
Deposit note: SMC does not have a deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. Historically, the majority of deposits are received later in the recruitment cycle. Current YTD deposits (11) are early-cycle indicators only and should not be compared directly to total prior-year figures (402–556). Year-over-year YTD comparison (+266.7%) is the meaningful metric at this stage.
Tracking note: On-campus tracks fall semester over fall semester. This is the first snapshot — 9/21/2026 is the baseline. Trend lines will build with each upload.

Why YTD deposits are not a concern

SMC does not have a deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. Historically, nearly all deposits are received later in the recruitment cycle — YTD figures at this point represent less than 1.5% of the final annual total. The current YTD deposit count of 11 is the highest of any prior year at this stage, which is a positive early signal.
