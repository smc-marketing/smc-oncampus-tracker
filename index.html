Fall 2027 semester · Fall-over-fall comparison · Last updated 11/09/2026

Fall 2027 active Deposits +4.5% Started apps -16.1%
Deposits still ahead of last year: 46 vs 44 (+4.5%). Inquiries had their best week of the tracked cycle (+72 vs +37 last year) and are now up 2.6% (1,803 vs 1,758). Created accounts without an app are double last year (21 vs 10), a leading indicator for future starts.
Accepts slipped this week. +108 accepts vs +187 for last year, widening the gap from -8.0% to -12.8% (1,160 vs 1,330). Started apps held at -16.1% and submitted slipped from -6.8% to -8.1%. Last year's accept jump may reflect a batch of decisions that week, so check next week before reading this as a trend.
Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. YTD deposits are an early-cycle indicator only; prior years finished with 402 to 495 current deposits, so 46 is about 10% of where the year will end.

Started apps — the persistent gap
-318 vs last year (-16.1%), essentially unchanged for three weeks. With inquiries up 2.6% and prospects down 16.2%, the shortfall is in new names entering the funnel, not inquiry-to-app conversion.
Conversion through the funnel is strong
93.5% of starts have submitted (1,546 of 1,654) vs 85.3% last year, which is why submitted (-8.1%) trails by far less than starts. 108 people have started but not submitted, the remarketing audience.

Gap is stable, not closing
The started-apps gap went from -22.2% on 9/28 to -16.2% by 10/26 and has held there for three weeks. Submitted briefly moved ahead on 10/12 (+1.1%) and has since slipped to -8.1%.
Watch accept-to-deposit yield
Fall 2026 finished with applications up 32% but deposits down 7.5%, because yield from accept to deposit fell from 24% to 16%. Volume is necessary but not sufficient; deposit conversion is the number to manage this spring.
