Fall 2027 active Submitted gap closing Started apps still down
Submitted apps gap closing fast — now just -4.5% vs last year. 637 submitted vs 667 last year. One week ago the gap was -7.7% (479 vs 519). The funnel conversion story is strengthening — students who start are completing and being admitted at an improving rate.
Started apps gap widened slightly to -22.2% (698 vs 897 last year). This is the persistent top-of-funnel concern. The remarketing campaign targeting started-not-submitted applicants is the right lever here — 61 people who started haven't submitted yet (698 started vs 637 submitted).
Deposit note: SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. Current deposits (15) are an early-cycle indicator only — the vast majority arrive later in the cycle. YTD deposits at +150% vs last year (15 vs 6) is a strong early signal. Historical context: prior years show less than 1.5% of final deposits come in by this date.

Why current deposits are not a concern — and actually a positive signal

SMC has no deposit deadline for on-campus enrollment. Historically less than 1.5% of final annual deposits come in by this point in the cycle (prior years: 7, 8, and 6 YTD vs 486–556 final totals). This year's 15 YTD deposits is the highest early-cycle count in the four-year window — a positive leading indicator, not a concern.
